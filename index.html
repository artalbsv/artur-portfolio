Bicycle e-commerce · Tray

Rodociclo
Bikeshop

Role
Product, Web & Visual Designer · Social Media Manager
Period
May 2025 — Present

One storefront.
Every screen considered.

A complete visual and functional reconstruction of an established bicycle e-commerce operation, designed and implemented within the constraints of the Tray platform.

Measured context ~R$10k→R$30–50k

The redesign contributed alongside marketing, commercial and operational initiatives to increasing monthly online sales from a previous maximum of approximately R$10,000 to between R$30,000 and R$50,000.

What moved through my hands

  • User experience and interface design
  • Responsive implementation
  • Tray platform customization
  • HTML, CSS, JavaScript and Twig
  • Customer purchasing flows
  • User and stakeholder feedback
  • Visual assets and product presentation
  • Social media content
  • AI-assisted design and development
Visit live website ↗
  1. 01

    Overview

    Rodociclo’s digital storefront was reconsidered as one connected customer journey, from product discovery to purchase, rather than a set of isolated visual updates.

  2. 02

    Initial challenge

    The established store needed clearer hierarchy, stronger visual consistency and a more dependable responsive experience while preserving day-to-day operations.

  3. 03

    My role

    I connected product and interface decisions with visual production, front-end customization and ongoing social content.

  4. 04

    Process

    I reviewed the live experience, prioritized high-impact purchasing flows, explored solutions and implemented improvements in progressive releases.

  5. 05

    Technical constraints

    The work was delivered within Tray’s theme architecture using HTML, CSS, JavaScript and Twig, with careful attention to maintainability and platform behavior.

  6. 06

    Feedback and validation

    Customer and stakeholder observations informed practical iterations across navigation, product presentation and purchasing flows.

  7. 07

    Final implementation

    The result is a responsive storefront and visual system that can support catalogue, promotional and operational needs over time.

  8. 08

    Outcome

    The redesign contributed alongside broader marketing, commercial and operational work to monthly online sales growing from a previous maximum of about R$10,000 to a range of R$30,000–R$50,000.
